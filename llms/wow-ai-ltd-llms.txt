# WOW AI

> WOW AI (Wow AI LTD., getwow.ai) is a smart e-commerce assistant for store owners in the Middle East and beyond. It connects to stores on Salla and Shopify, analyzes sales, customer, and product data, and delivers daily insights, ready-made recommendations, smart promotional offers, inventory and pricing alerts, and an AI chat assistant that understands Arabic and English. Backed by 500 Global. No public developer API is published as of 2026-07-21; the api.getwow.ai backend is private/undocumented.
